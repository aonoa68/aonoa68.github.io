Course code 8148

Statistics II

Builds the foundations of statistical inference with conditional probability, Bayes’ theorem, and probability distributions. Students then use simulation, information cascades, and Condorcet’s jury theorem to show how conformity breaks the independence of trials and degrades group decisions.
